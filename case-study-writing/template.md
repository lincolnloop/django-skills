# Lincoln Loop Case Study Template 2024

## Interview Script

Use these questions when interviewing the user (skip anything already known from the codebase or git history):

1. What problems, pain points, and needs did the client have at the start of the project?
2. What was the client looking to achieve?
3. How was Lincoln Loop uniquely fit to solve the client's problem? (ex: previous experience, how we collaborated, our approach, our service offerings, etc.)
4. How did we solve their problem? (ex: our approach, technologies used, etc.)
5. Did our solution include anything innovative or a unique problem-solving technique?
6. What challenges did we face when arriving at the solution?
7. Did we bring anything to the project that went beyond client expectations and offered value-add?
8. What are the notable successes and achievements of the project?
9. What long term benefits did our solution and approach provide?
10. Do we have any metrics or statistics we can pull that would highlight their initial pain points and later improvements?
11. What are the future plans or next steps for the project?
12. Do you have any ideas around what visuals would be good to support the content of the case study?
13. Is there anything you'd like to mention that we haven't already discussed?

## Case Study Structure

```markdown
# [Project Name] Case Study

## When [client] needed to [challenge], Lincoln Loop [high-level solution].

[Example: When NOLS needed to improve their website's search functionality, Lincoln Loop designed a faceted search and component-based CMS.]

**Client:** [Enter client's name]
**Project URL:** [Enter project URL, if applicable]
**Duration:** [Enter length]
**Industries:** [Examples: technology, energy, health, manufacturing, education, etc.]
**Services:** [Examples: django development, design systems, data migration, etc.]
**Technology:** [Examples: django, python, AWS, etc.]

## Background

[Provide a more in-depth overview of the client. Talk about their background, industry, and any recent successes or milestones they have had. Aim to provide a concise yet informative overview that helps readers understand the context of the project and the client's needs.]

## Challenge

[Explain the challenge the client faced, including specific problems, pain points and needs. Set the stage for understanding why the project was necessary and what it aimed to achieve. Consider including metrics and stats to highlight the severity of the challenge. End with the specific outcomes the client wanted to achieve. If applicable, this section can also be written as an "Opportunity" instead of a challenge.]

## The Right Fit for the Job

[Explain why Lincoln Loop was uniquely fit to solve the client's challenge. This can include our previous experience, client collaboration, project approach, full-service capabilities, etc.]

## Solution

[Explain how Lincoln Loop solved the client's challenge. How did the client feel about working with us? Depending on the scenario, you may include details around approach, implementation, technologies used, client collaboration, and deployment. Be sure to highlight any innovative approaches or problem-solving techniques.]

## Results

[This is an opportunity to celebrate the main successes and achievements of the project. Highlight the outcomes and benefits achieved through the project. This section should be data-driven and focus on the impact of the solution. The data available will depend on the project, but some opportunities to consider include: metrics, before & after comparisons, client feedback, user test results, operational efficiency, financial impact, and awards.]

## Looking Forward

[Outline future plans, potential next steps, and long-term benefits resulting from the project. Aim to demonstrate the ongoing value of the solution, reinforcing the long-term benefits of partnering with Lincoln Loop.]

## Stats and Metrics

[If you have any stats and metrics that exist to support the content of the case study, capture them here.]

## Quotes and Testimonials

[If you have any quotes or testimonials from the client or our team to support the content of the case study, capture them here.]

## Visuals

[If you have any art direction around what visuals exist to support the content of the case study, capture them here.]
```
